**Author:** Muhammad Hasnaat Anjum, Department of Computer Science, University of Sahiwal
**Date:** October 2026 · Working paper

An analytical essay on how generative AI tools affect student productivity —
what the recent empirical evidence actually shows, where quality fails to

## Abstract

Generative AI tools have become routine in student academic life, yet their
net effect on productivity remains contested. Drawing on recent
meta-analyses and empirical studies, this paper argues that AI tools
reliably accelerate mechanical aspects of academic work — drafting,
debugging, summarizing — while their effect on learning quality is uneven
and depends on discipline, guidance, and the student's verification effort.
